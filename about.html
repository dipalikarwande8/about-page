ABOUT UTHAN TABLA ACADEMY

Preserving Tradition.
Creating Musicians.

Uthan Tabla Academy is dedicated to nurturing the art of Tabla through disciplined learning, traditional knowledge and meaningful musical expression.

01 OUR ACADEMY

The rhythm of tradition, the spirit of learning.

Uthan Tabla Academy is a dedicated space for students who wish to explore the rich tradition of Hindustani classical music through the Tabla.

Our approach combines the discipline of traditional Tabla education with practical performance experience. Students are encouraged to develop strong fundamentals, rhythmic understanding, listening skills and confidence in performance.

From beginners taking their first steps into Tabla to dedicated students developing advanced techniques, the academy provides a structured environment for continuous musical growth.

01

Traditional Learning

Learn the fundamentals, bols, taal and compositions through a systematic approach rooted in the Tabla tradition.

02

Performance

Develop practical performance skills and understand how Tabla interacts with different forms of Indian classical music.

03

Individual Guidance

Students receive focused guidance to build technique, rhythm, musicality and confidence.
